detent.

a home screen for Android that you turn.

Your apps sit on a dial at the edge of the screen. Turn the dial to find an app. Tap the app to open it.

download the APK source on GitHub

latest release · Android 10 or later · MIT

soft · radial · double
dusk

Drag, flick, or scroll the dial. Tap a name to open it. Hold the dial to jump by letter.

use the dial.

drag or flick the dial
The dial turns. Each app clicks into the notch.
tap any app on the dial
The app opens. The dial turns the app into the notch.
tap the dial while it turns
The dial stops. No app opens.
hold the dial
Letters replace the apps. Slide to a letter and release to jump to it.
hold the app in the notch
A menu shows the app shortcuts, app info, and remove from dial.
hold the clock
The setup opens: shape, theme, apps, and feel.
drag up from the bottom
Start the drag at the apps hint. Search all your apps. The 5 apps that you last opened from search sit at the top. Tap the cross on a recent app to remove it, or tap clear. Tap + dial to add an app to the dial.
tap a quick button
Its app opens. The quick buttons sit in the bottom corner on the dial side and hold up to 3 apps, such as the camera. Each button shows an icon that you pick. With 1 quick app, the button can show the app name.
hold a quick button
A menu shows the app shortcuts, change icon, change app, add a quick app, and remove quick app.
tap the mic button
The mic button sits at the screen edge. Say an app name. The dial swells with your voice, and your words show at the top. When the words name one app, the dial turns to it and the app opens. If not, search opens with the words. To hide the mic button, turn off voice on the feel step.
tap the web button
Type words and press Enter. Your default browser searches the web with its own search engine. If the browser takes no web search, a search app such as Google does it. Detent only hands the words over. To hide the web button, turn off web search on the feel step.
drag down from the top
The notifications open. On the feel step, pick detent bar to hide the Android status bar. Detent then shows only wifi, signal, and battery, in the style of the layout.
a card on the home screen
Detent suggests changes to the dial. If you open an app from search 3 times in a week, the card asks you to add it. If you do not open a dial app for 3 weeks, the card asks you to remove it. Tap not now or keep to hide the card for that app.
j k Enter
With a hardware keyboard, j and k turn the dial. Enter opens the app.

The demo on this page does the first 4 gestures, a tap on the quick button, and the keys. Your scroll wheel also turns it.

8 layouts.

Layouts 1 to 5 put a radial dial over the wallpaper. Layouts 6 to 8 draw the whole screen as one grid of dots. On those, the clock is a dot-matrix readout. Tap a layout to load it in the demo.

On the grid layouts, an app opens with a ripple of dots from the notch. Each layout can sit on the right or the left edge.

27 themes.

The 4 phone themes use a tinted dot-hand art. The 23 Omarchy themes each bring their own wallpaper. Tap a theme to apply it to the demo and to this page.

You can also use your own photo as the wallpaper. The theme then sets only the colours.

no internet permission.

Detent does not have the internet permission. It does not send data anywhere.

permissionwhy Detent needs it
QUERY_ALL_PACKAGESTo list the apps you can open and to find your default apps for the first dial.
VIBRATEFor haptics.
SET_WALLPAPERFor the lock screen switch.
EXPAND_STATUS_BARTo open the notifications when you drag down from the top of the home screen.
ACCESS_NETWORK_STATEFor the wifi signal in the detent bar. Detent reads only the signal strength.
RECORD_AUDIOOptional. For the mic button. The microphone is on only while Detent listens. The speech service of your phone turns your voice into text.
notification accessOptional. For unread dots. Detent reads only which apps have notifications. It does not keep the notification text.

install.

You need Android 10 or later.

  1. Download detent.apk from the latest release.
  2. Open the file on your phone.
  3. If Android asks, allow your browser or file manager to install apps.
  4. Open Detent and follow the setup.
  5. On the ready step, tap set as home app.

To install from a computer, connect the phone with USB debugging on and run this command:

adb install detent.apk

To build from source, read the README.
